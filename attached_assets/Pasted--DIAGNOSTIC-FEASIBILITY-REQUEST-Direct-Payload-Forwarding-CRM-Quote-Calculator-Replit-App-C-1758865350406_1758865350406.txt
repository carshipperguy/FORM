Desired change (diagnosis only): Instead of sending the payload to Zapier, can the CRM app also (or instead) forward the same payload directly to our other Replit Quote Calculator app endpoint? Payload must include all marketing tracking info (UTM fields, fbclid, CAPI data, session id) and the full lead data.

Important: Do not disable or remove existing Zapier integrations during analysis. This request is diagnostic/feasibility-only. No implementation or production changes allowed until we explicitly approve.
