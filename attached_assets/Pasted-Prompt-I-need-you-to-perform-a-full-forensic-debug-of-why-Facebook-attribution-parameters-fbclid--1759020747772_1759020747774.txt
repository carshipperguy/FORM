Verify the parameters appear in the landing page URL.

Ensure no legacy hardcoded dev URLs are still used in attribution calls.

Make sure Zapier field mapping includes fbclid + all utm_* fields and isn’t discarding them.

Are fbclid and utm params being captured correctly in the browser session?

Are they being handed off correctly from parent → iframe?

Are they included in the webhook payload sent to Zapier?

Focus on actual root cause (not just “it works in dev”), and guarantee that production payloads contain fbclid + all utm_ values*.
